## the suitors

while odysseus was gone from ithaca, suitors filled his halls. eating his stores, courting his wife, certain he would never return. they mistook an empty house for an open one.

my ithaca is not empty. it's tended and lived in, not left unguarded for anyone passing through to help themselves to.

i am not accepting pull requests, issues, or contributions of any kind to this repository. this code is not licensed for reuse. see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## what you can do

- look around. read the code if you're curious how something works.

## what you can't do

- open a pull request (it will be closed without review)
- fork this for your own use. the license doesn't permit it
- open issues expecting a response

no bows to string here. no axes to shoot through.
